# Freshman Resume Template

A simple one-page LaTeX resume template for first- and second-year undergraduate students.

一个适合大一、大二本科生使用的一页式 LaTeX 简历模板。

This template is mainly designed for students applying to:

该模板主要适用于申请：

- Research labs / 科研实验室
- Technical programs / 技术项目
- Internships / 实习
- Engineering teams / 工程团队
- Student organizations / 学生技术组织

---

## Why this template? / 为什么做这个模板？

Many first- and second-year students do not yet have long internship or research histories. A good resume at this stage should focus less on the number of experiences and more on clearly showing what you have done, what skills you have used, and what you are capable of learning next.

很多大一、大二学生还没有很长的实习或科研经历。这个阶段的简历不需要堆很多内容，更重要的是让别人快速看懂：你做过什么、实际用过哪些技能，以及你有没有继续培养的潜力。

The template therefore keeps the structure simple and focuses on four sections:

因此，这份模板采用了比较简单的四部分结构：

- Education / 教育经历
- Experience / 实践经历
- Projects / 项目经历
- Skills / 技能

---

## How to Use / 如何使用

1. Download `resume_template.tex`  
   下载 `resume_template.tex`

2. Upload it to Overleaf, or open it with your local LaTeX environment  
   上传到 Overleaf，或者使用本地 LaTeX 环境打开

3. Replace all placeholder information with your own experience  
   将模板中的占位内容替换成你自己的信息

4. Remove sections or projects that are not relevant to you  
   删除与你无关的部分或项目

5. Compile using pdfLaTeX  
   使用 pdfLaTeX 编译

---

## Education / 教育经历

For first-year students, it is completely reasonable to keep high school information, especially if your university experience is still limited or your high school academic record is strong.

对于刚进入大学的学生来说，保留高中经历是完全正常的。尤其是在大学经历还不多，或者高中成绩、课程体系、学术表现比较突出的情况下，高中部分仍然有价值。

As you gain more university experience, you can gradually remove high school information and give more space to university research, projects, coursework, and internships.

随着大学经历逐渐增加，可以慢慢删掉高中部分，把更多空间留给大学阶段的科研、项目、课程和实习。

---

## Experience / 实践经历

The Experience section should focus on the environment you worked in, your responsibilities, collaboration, and how you contributed to a team or organization.

Experience 部分更适合写你在什么环境中做事、承担了什么责任、如何和别人合作，以及你如何参与一个团队或组织。

Try to avoid repeating the same technical details that already appear in the Projects section.

如果一个项目已经会在 Projects 里详细介绍技术实现，那么 Experience 里就尽量不要重复同样的内容。

---

## Projects / 项目经历

For technical and research-oriented resumes, Projects are often one of the most important sections.

对于技术和科研方向的简历来说，Projects 往往是最重要的部分之一。

A strong project description should make it clear:

一个好的项目描述最好能够让别人快速看懂：

- What you built / 你做了什么
- What tools or methods you used / 你用了什么技术或方法
- How you tested, evaluated, or improved it / 你如何测试、评估或改进它

Instead of writing:

不要只写：

> Worked on a programming project.

Try to write something more specific:

可以写得更具体：

> Built a web application for tracking course assignments, implementing deadline filtering and task-priority features.

The goal is not to make every project sound extremely advanced. The goal is to show that you actually understand what you built.

重点不是让每个项目听起来都很高级，而是让别人看得出你确实理解自己做的东西。

---

## Research Projects / 科研型项目

Research projects do not need to have perfect or positive results.

科研项目不一定要有非常漂亮或者完全正向的结果。

If you designed an experiment, compared different conditions, analyzed failures, or tested a hypothesis, these are already meaningful research skills.

如果你设计了实验、比较了不同条件、分析了失败案例，或者检验了一个假设，这些本身就是有价值的科研能力。

Focus on explaining the research question, experimental setup, and evaluation process clearly.

重点是把研究问题、实验设计和评估方法写清楚。

---
